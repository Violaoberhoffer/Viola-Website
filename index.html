How does audiobook pricing work? +

Priced per finished hour

Audiobooks are priced per finished hour – the length of the final audiobook, not the hours of work behind it. As a rule of thumb, about 9,300 words make one finished hour.

Your bookAudiobook lengthNarration onlyComplete package
60,000 words≈ 6.5 hours≈ €1,625≈ €2,600
80,000 words≈ 8.5 hours≈ €2,125≈ €3,400
100,000 words≈ 11 hours≈ €2,750≈ €4,400

Examples are estimates. You’ll get an exact quote based on your manuscript.

Narration only

Preparation, recording in my studio, corrections, and clean raw files per chapter – for publishers and producers with their own post-production.

Complete package

Everything in “Narration only”, plus professional editing, proof-listening, mastering to Audible/ACX standards, one round of corrections, and finished files ready to upload.

How it works

  1. Send me a request with your book’s title, genre and word count.
  2. You get a quote and a proposed schedule.
  3. I record your audiobook chapter by chapter.
  4. You listen and send me your corrections (one round included).
  5. You receive the finished files.

What I need from you

Your final, proofread manuscript, a short list of how names and invented words are pronounced, and a few notes on your main characters.

About

Hi, I’m Viola

I’m a dreamer through and through – I never stopped dreaming. I’ve wanted to be an author since I was twelve, when the Twilight saga inspired me to start writing. I discovered how much I love diving into my own worlds and creating something boundless with nothing but my thoughts and ideas.

Acting has always had a place in my heart, too. For years, I recorded what I’d written just for myself, and I realized how much I love voicing emotional moments. Three years ago, I started training as a voice actor – because lending my voice to a story is what truly connects it all for me.

I write when my kids are in bed and it gets dark outside, in my cozy writing corner full of little things – a corner that looks just like me. I’m married, a mom of two, and we’re a little American family that happens to live in Germany. My stories are set in the US, and I’ve dreamed of living there since I was a teenager.

More than anything, I want to inspire others to follow their dreams. And to every artist out there: you are not alone.

“Artists are the living, breathing souls on whose backs the beauty of humanity is born.”
